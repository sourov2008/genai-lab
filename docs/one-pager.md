# ClauseDesk — Product One-Pager

## 1. Problem (One paragraph)

Small businesses, freelancers, and startup founders who don't have an in-house lawyer often skip reading full contracts. Reading an 8 to 50-page agreement takes over 40 minutes, and even then, they usually miss hidden traps. As a result, they either sign blindly and face legal risks, or they lose business opportunities out of fear. Hiring a lawyer to review every contract is too expensive for them.


## 2. User (One line)

Freelance software developers and small 10-20 person startups who receive 2-5 contracts a month but have no internal legal team to review them.
